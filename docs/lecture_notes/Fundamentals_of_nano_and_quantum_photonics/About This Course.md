## Course Overview
The course objective is to familiarize students with research frontiers in Nano and Quantum Optics. It covers fundamental principles with an emphasis on building intuitive understanding and developing analytical techniques across 12 structured modules.

---

## Syllabus & Weekly Breakdown

### Part I: Classical & Plasmonic Nanophotonics
- [ ] **Week 1: Introduction to Nanophotonics** — Why nanophotonics? Electromagnetics review, Maxwell equations, Wave optics, Evanescent waves, Diffraction limit.
- [ ] **Week 2: Light-Matter Interaction** — Dielectric function, Kramers-Kronig relations, Drude-Lorentz and Drude models, Interband & Intraband transitions.
- [ ] **Week 3: Plasmonics** — Quasi-static limit, Nanoparticle as a plasmonic atom, Size-dependent absorption/scattering, Plasmon hybridization.
- [ ] **Week 4: Dielectric Nanophotonics** — Photonics in 2D, 1D, and 0D semiconductors, Selection rules, Photonic density of states.

### Part II: Periodic Structures & Metasurfaces
- [ ] **Week 5: 1D Periodic Potential** — Scattering from planar interfaces, Photonic bandgaps, Rayleigh anomalies.
- [ ] **Week 6: 2D Periodic Potential & Metasurfaces** — Electric/magnetic metamaterials, Negative refractive index, Superlens & hyperlens, Plasmonic and Dielectric metasurfaces.
- [ ] **Week 7: Active Metamaterials** — Optical gain, Radiative/non-radiative transitions, Amplified emission, Lasing threshold, Nano-lasers.
- [ ] **Week 8: Nanofabrication & Transition** — Device examples from literature, Transition from classical to quantum nanophotonics.

### Part III: Quantum Optics & Field Quantization
- [ ] **Week 9: Photon Statistics** — Quantum regime photonics, Classification by photon statistics, Super-Poissonian/Sub-Poissonian light, Photo-detection.
- [ ] **Week 10: Photon Anti-Bunching** — Hanbury-Brown Twiss interferometer, Second-order correlation function $g^{(2)}(\tau)$, Single-photon sources.
- [ ] **Week 11: Canonical Quantization** — Quantum harmonic oscillator, Quadratures, Vacuum fluctuations, Coherent states, Uncertainty.
- [ ] **Week 12: Resonant Light-Atom Interactions** — Time-dependent Schrödinger equation, Strong/weak coupling, Rabi oscillations.
